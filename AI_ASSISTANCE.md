## Scope of AI-assisted content

AI-assisted generation or substantial revision affects the application,
configuration, analysis, scoring, ML/training code, data services, map and
document interface, and automated tests. Each implementation/test module identifies
this assistance in its opening source comment and refers to this record.
The attribution covers the module as a whole; it does not claim that every
line was generated by AI or that unmarked lines were written independently.

The exact line-by-line provenance of the inherited prototype has not been
reconstructed. ChatGPT's earlier assistance is therefore disclosed here rather
than assigned to particular current lines without evidence. When adding
independently written or externally adapted code, document that provenance
beside the affected section instead of relying only on the general header.

AI also assisted with the README, technical explanations, this record and the
project proposal/email wording. The trained model uses official MeteoSwiss
observations, not invented AI training values. Model predictions are outputs
of the project's training/inference code, not facts supplied by a language model.
Official forms and datasets retain their own source attribution.
